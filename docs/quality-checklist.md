# Handover quality and privacy checklist

Before considering the plan ready:

- [ ] Leave dates, role and coverage period are accurate (estimated dates marked clearly).
- [ ] Source coverage is disclosed; inaccessible tools were not claimed as reviewed.
- [ ] Each critical recurring task and open project has a **confirmed** coverage owner and backup where needed.
- [ ] Temporary decision-makers and escalation thresholds are approved by the manager.
- [ ] Team support and reporting lines (if relevant) are agreed and communicated.
- [ ] Meeting coverage, documentation and upcoming deadlines are confirmed.
- [ ] Access requests are approved through normal IT/security processes; **no passwords** are shared.
- [ ] Handover training/shadowing is completed and documented where needed.
- [ ] Contact boundaries during leave are explicitly agreed, without requiring availability.
- [ ] Return/reverse-handover plan is considered.
- [ ] Undocumented history and context have been captured or discussed.
- [ ] No medical, compensation, performance, immigration, or unrelated private employee data is included.
- [ ] Every proposed owner is labeled as a suggestion until confirmed.
- [ ] Final document location and audience are approved by the employee and manager.

The AI draft is not an HR policy, an authorization to delegate, or evidence that an access change occurred.

- [ ] The exact Notion destination and intended audience are established before workplace content is written.
- [ ] The agent returned the actual page link and disclosed any readback or write failure.
- [ ] The initial page is a draft with separate employee and manager review states.
- [ ] Employee edits are preserved on later revisions.
- [ ] No duplicate page was created after a partial failure.
