# Customize the workflow

| Setting | What to choose |
| --- | --- |
| Role | Individual contributor or people manager |
| Sources | Approved, connected work sources, or NONE for supplied notes |
| Reporting window | A bounded recent period plus upcoming commitments during leave |
| Notion destination | Exact authorized parent-page or database URL, or NONE |
| Audience | Intended employee and reviewers; verify inherited access |
| Review process | Who accepts coverage and confirms decision authority |
| Lead time | Adapt to the role, risk and available notice |
| Output | Notion draft by default; chat or local HTML as fallback |

Use this for sabbaticals, medical leave, parental leave, and other extended absences without asking the employee for medical or personal reasons. Adapt preparation to the available notice, including sudden or unplanned absences. Keep Context only I know as an employee contribution, not an AI inference.
