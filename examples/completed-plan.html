Your leave at a glance

A MOMENT TO REFLECT

The invisible work matters, too

Not all of your contributions show up in a task list. Consider these questions before completing the plan.

Reflection prompts
  • Where do people use you as a tiebreaker because ownership is unclear?
  • Where do you routinely spot dependencies others miss?
  • Which functions or projects do you connect?
  • Who relies on you to challenge their thinking?
  • Where do you give someone confidence to make a difficult call?
  • Which meetings are materially better because you are there?
  • Which relationships depend on your context, trust, or credibility?

Context only you know

The history, workarounds and unwritten rules that only you hold. Capture the reason things are done a certain way, not just the steps.

Prompts to help you remember
  • What would a new person get wrong because they do not know the history?
  • Which decisions were made for reasons that are not written down?
  • Which customers, vendors or colleagues have quirks, commitments or past issues the cover person should know about?
  • Which workarounds, manual fixes or one-off agreements would stop working without you?
  • What has already been tried and did not work?
